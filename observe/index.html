Part 1 · Learn

How to Collect Both Measures

The goal is to keep your eyes on the teacher-student interaction and make the electronic form require as little attention as possible.

1

Review first

Before every observation, read the student behavior definition and the individualized teacher-fidelity targets. Know what you are looking for before the timer begins.

2

Student behavior

Student behavior uses 15-second partial-interval recording. If the target occurs at any point in the current interval, tap the red button once. No tap means it did not occur.

3

Teacher fidelity

When a fidelity opportunity occurs, make one quick selection: Implemented as Written or Not Implemented as Written. Items with no opportunity are resolved after the session.

4

15-second cue

The cue only means a new interval began. It is not momentary time sampling. Keep watching continuously for student behavior across the interval.

5

Implemented? Not implemented? No opportunity?

Implemented as Written

The observable plan component occurred as operationally defined.

Not Implemented as Written

An opportunity occurred, but the plan component was absent or did not meet its definition.

No Opportunity

The event needed to score the component never occurred during the observation.

If you select Implemented as Written: also record whether the strategy had the desired outcome on behavior — Yes, No, or Not clear.

6

Independent scoring

When two observers are present, collect independently. Do not compare or discuss scores until both records are submitted.
