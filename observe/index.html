Part 1 · Learn

One Observation, Two Things to Watch

You are watching the teacher-student interaction. The form is designed so you can record both measures with as few taps as possible.

1
Student behavior

If the target behavior happens at any point during the current 15-second interval, tap the red button once.

2
Teacher fidelity

When a fidelity opportunity occurs, make one quick score for that teacher behavior.

BEFOREKnow exactly what you are watching for

Review the student behavior definition, examples/non-examples, and the individualized teacher-fidelity targets before the observation begins.

You should not be learning the definitions while you are trying to collect data.

DURINGWatch the interaction and make quick marks

Student behavior

15-second partial interval: if the target occurs at any point in the interval, tap the red button once.

No tap= behavior did not occur
15-sec cue= a new interval started

The cue is not telling you when to look at the student. Keep observing across the whole interval.

Teacher fidelity

When the opportunity for a plan component occurs, score it right then:

Implemented as Written The observable plan component occurred as defined.
Not Implemented as Written An opportunity occurred, but the component was absent or did not meet the definition.

If implemented: also indicate whether it had the desired outcome — Yes, No, or Not clear.

AFTERResolve anything left blank and submit
No Opportunity Use this only when the event needed to score that fidelity target never occurred during the observation.
Then submit Once submitted, your training record is saved for agreement comparison.

When another observer is present: collect independently. Do not compare or discuss scores until both people have submitted.
